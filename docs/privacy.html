Privacy policy

Jero (Jeroboam) has no accounts to create and no analytics or advertising trackers. Your progress (streak, XP and completed lessons), your journal, your cellar and your photos are stored on your device and, while “Sync with iCloud” is on (the default), in your own private iCloud, which we cannot read. You can turn syncing off, and Settings > Erase all my data deletes everything from your iPhone and from iCloud.

Wines you scan

When you scan or look up a wine, the app sends the label text (or, if the text is unclear, the label photo) to Anthropic's Claude to identify the wine, so it can show you details and a price. Your photo is not kept by us.

To make the app better and cheaper to run, the wine itself (its name, producer, vintage, region, country, grapes, style, tasting notes and price) is also saved in our own wine database and counted, so we can see which wines people look up and so the next person who scans the same wine gets the answer instantly. This is anonymous: it contains no name, email, account or photo, and it is not linked to you. When you scan a label with the camera, the app also sends a small numeric fingerprint of the label's look (made on your phone by Apple's Vision framework), so the same label can be recognised faster next time; it is a list of numbers, not the photo, and the photo cannot be rebuilt from it. Wine lookups go through our own small server, which carries a random number the app makes on first use, so the server can keep each phone to a small daily allowance of lookups, and the same number is sent with each scan so we can count how many phones use the app each day; it is not your device's advertising identifier and says nothing about you. The prices you type in as "what you paid" stay on your device.

If you turn on daily reminders, they are scheduled locally on your iPhone. No account, analytics or tracking is used.

Privacy policy

Jero (Jeroboam) has no accounts to create and no analytics or advertising trackers. Your progress (streak, XP and completed lessons), your journal, your cellar and your photos are stored on your device and, if you sign in with Apple, in your own iCloud.

Wines you scan

When you scan or look up a wine, the app sends the label text (or, if the text is unclear, the label photo) to Anthropic's Claude to identify the wine, so it can show you details and a price. Your photo is not kept by us.

To make the app better and cheaper to run, the wine itself (its name, producer, vintage, region, country, grapes, style, tasting notes and price) is also saved in our own wine database and counted, so we can see which wines people look up and so the next person who scans the same wine gets the answer instantly. This is anonymous: it contains no name, email, account, device identifier or photo, and it is not linked to you. The prices you type in as "what you paid" stay on your device.

If you turn on daily reminders, they are scheduled locally on your iPhone. No account, analytics or tracking is used.

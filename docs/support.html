Jeroboam support

Jeroboam teaches wine through short daily lessons, from the basics to advanced study, with an atlas of grapes, regions and aromas.

Common questions

How do I start at a higher level? Tap a locked level on the Learn tab and choose “Unlock”.

How do streaks work? Finish at least one lesson or practice session a day. Every 7 days you earn a streak freeze that covers one missed day.

Reset progress? Profile → Settings → Reset progress.

For anything else, please open an issue on GitHub.
